## 📁 Overview

The fallhome-law-research-hub serves as a workspace for legal professionals. It manages case files, tracks conflicts, and allows for local research without an internet connection. This software stores all firm data in one file. This design keeps your records private and secure on your own local device. You handle US law research and internal firm tracking through a browser interface that functions offline.

## 🛠️ How to Use the Hub

Open the program by double-clicking the desktop icon. Since this tool acts as a browser-native app, it opens a window that looks just like your favorite web browser. 

### Starting a New Project
Once you open the software, click the New Case button to start a fresh project. You can import existing documents or type your notes directly into the text fields. 

### Offline Research
You do not need an active internet connection to search your loaded legal documents. Type your keywords into the search bar at the top of the interface. The tool scans your local files and shows results in seconds.

### Conflict Tracking
Use the Conflict tab to enter the names of current clients and opposing parties. The system checks these names against your database every time you start a new search. This feature prevents you from entering cases where a conflict exists.

### Saving Data
This tool saves data automatically as you type. Because the entire system operates out of a single file, you can move, copy, or back up your entire firm database by simply moving that one file to a secure drive or cloud backup service.

## 📋 Frequently Asked Questions

### Does this software store my data on the internet?
No. All data stays on your local hard drive. The application does not connect to external servers to sync your files.

### Can I share files with my team?
Yes. You can share the single source file with your colleagues. Ensure only one person opens the file at a time to prevent errors in data recording.

### What if I need to update the app?
Download the newest version from the link provided above and run the installer again. The update process ignores your existing data file, so your research stays safe.

### Is there a limit to how many cases I can store?
The tool handles very large databases. Performance depends on the speed of your computer hard drive. We recommend regular backups to keep your system fast and responsive.

## 🛡️ Security and Privacy

Your firm handles sensitive information. This tool protects your data by keeping it local. No third party ever accesses your case notes or client list. We provide no remote login doors or back-end connections. Your computer holds the only copy of your sensitive documents. You control who sees your work.
